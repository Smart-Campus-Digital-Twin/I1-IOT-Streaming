Here's the challenge: A modern university has hundreds of sensors — temperature in classrooms, occupancy in hallways, vibration in equipment rooms. Each sensor streams data independently. How do we make sense of it all? How do we detect when something goes wrong *before* it becomes a problem?

In the next 4 minutes, we'll walk through:

- How we detect problems automatically  
- What insights we unlock with this data

Let's dive in.
